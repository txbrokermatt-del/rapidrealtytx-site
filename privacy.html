Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 22, 2026

Rapid Realty LLC ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you interact with us through our website, advertisements, lead generation forms, and SMS communications.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a lead form, request information, or opt in to communications from us, we may collect:

  • Your full name
  • Your mobile phone number
  • Your email address
  • Your property preferences (e.g., buying, selling, location, budget)
  • Timestamp of your submission and consent
  • Communication history with our team

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your property inquiries
  • Schedule appointments and consultations
  • Send appointment confirmations and reminders
  • Provide property updates and listings relevant to your interests
  • Follow up on your inquiry by phone, SMS, or email
  • Improve our services

3. SMS Messaging Program

By providing your phone number and checking the consent box on our lead generation forms, you expressly consent to receive recurring SMS text messages from Rapid Realty LLC. These messages may include appointment confirmations, property information, follow-up communications, and scheduling reminders.

  • Message frequency varies depending on your interaction with our team.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message.
  • You can request help by replying HELP to any message.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

4. Sharing of Information

Rapid Realty LLC does not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number, SMS consent, or personal information with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Your information is used solely by Rapid Realty LLC and its authorized agents to communicate with you regarding your real estate inquiry.

We may share information only when:

  • Required by law or legal process
  • Necessary to complete a real estate transaction you have requested (e.g., with title companies, lenders, or licensed cooperating brokers)
  • You have given us explicit permission to do so

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. All consent records and communication logs are stored securely.

6. Your Rights

You may:

  • Request access to the information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your information
  • Opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP
  • Withdraw your consent for future communications

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the information below.
